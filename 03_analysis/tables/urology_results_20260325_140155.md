## Key Finding: The Boot Camp Opportunity

The intersection of LLMs, urology, and simulation boot camps represents
a significant research gap. Current boot camps rely on human standardized
patients or mannequins with human operators speaking through speakers.
LLM-powered text-to-speech could replace the human operator, enabling:
- Scalable, location-independent training
- Consistent patient responses across sessions
- 24/7 availability without human scheduling constraints
- Multilingual support
- Automated assessment and feedback
