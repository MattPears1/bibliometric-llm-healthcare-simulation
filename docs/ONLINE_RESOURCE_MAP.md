Online Resource 6 has five sections:

1. Pilot: 100 agent workflow-test records.
2. Eligibility Sample 1: August lead-author check of 100 records.
3. Eligibility Sample 2: September lead-author check of 100 records, preserving answer stages.
4. Recheck of 15 original August disagreements.
5. NTS classification check: 44 selected records, including 38 studies in the frozen corpus.

Resource 6 section numbers refer to these five folders, not to manuscript section numbers. No record is silently counted as an independent additional human review. Pilot and human-sample identifiers must be joined by paper_id, because the original Pilot and August sample both used H-prefixed audit IDs.
