A controlled non-technical-skills thesaurus comprising 78 concept groups and 303 exact lexical variants was applied to all 483 study titles and the 447 available abstracts. Text was Unicode-normalised and lowercased, with punctuation and hyphenation standardised. Exact phrase matching and binary whole counting were used; no stemming or fuzzy matching was applied. Same-study co-occurrence was calculated for the 18 most frequent eligible concepts.
