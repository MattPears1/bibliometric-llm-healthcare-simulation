ONLINE RESOURCE 1 - SEARCH METHODS

18 exact searches across six sources. The eligibility window is 1 January 2020 to 13 August 2026. Five sources were searched on 14 August; Scopus on 16 August. Retrieval occurrences precede deduplication and are not unique studies. No new live search was conducted for this release.

CANDIDATE RULE
A deduplicated record entered the candidate frame when its complete saved title, abstract, or source keywords contained evidence for all four prespecified concepts: (1) a language model or generative-language system; (2) healthcare; (3) education or training; and (4) simulation. Prespecified names, spelling variants, and related phrases were used. The rule defined the candidate frame and did not determine final eligibility.

FILE FIELDS
source and query_id identify each query; execution_date records when it ran; eligible_from/eligible_through define the publication window; fields_and_settings and query_text preserve the source-specific search; retrieval_occurrences is the query-level return count; status records the saved execution status.
