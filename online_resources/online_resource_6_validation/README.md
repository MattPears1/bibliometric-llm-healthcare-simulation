| Section | Records and purpose |

| 2. Eligibility Sample 1 | August 100-record lead-author check, initial comparisons and recovered original rationales |

There was one lead author performing the human checks. The Pilot was an AI-agent pipeline test and must not be counted as human review. Its 23-record overlap with the August human sample is verified from paper identifiers. The two human eligibility samples share five records, giving 195 unique identifiers across those two samples; the NTS check is a subset of the September sample. No pooled accuracy estimate is made.

## Separate answer stages

The August initial check agreed with the then-current five-source pipeline on 85/100 records. It predates the Scopus extension and later eligibility corrections. The planned second-human adjudication was not completed. Later rechecking does not overwrite the original answers.

In the September sample, 80 records were inside the candidate frame and 20 were outside it. After clarification of blank answers, 65/78 determinate in-frame answers matched the frozen decisions; two remained uncertain. After comparison, 71/80 matched and nine differed. One of the 20 outside-frame records remained included by the author. Missing final rationales are explicitly recorded rather than reconstructed.

The NTS group was selected as the union of frozen and latest-author inclusions within the September sample. It is not an independent random sample of 44 from the 483 studies. For the 38 corpus studies, 305 of 342 domain judgements agreed, 22 differed and 15 were unclear. Agreement among 327 determinate judgements was 93.27%. The other classification worksheet contained 352 blank cells, so no validation of every extraction variable is claimed.

These later differences are disclosed alongside the frozen 483-study analysis. This release does not adjudicate them or silently replace corpus membership or labels. No human-reference-standard accuracy, sensitivity or specificity claim is made.

## Privacy and interpretation

Abstracts, source keywords, downloaded full texts, personal paths and original review workbooks are excluded. Professional publication titles, identifiers, links and original review rationales are retained. Original template labels and dated protocols must be read with the process explanations: a column called human_decision does not prove that a human completed an agent dry run.

Use FIELD GUIDE.txt and the section-specific summaries for field definitions. The historical folder name SAMPLE2 refers to the August human sample, now called Eligibility Sample 1; it is distinct from the September Eligibility Sample 2.
