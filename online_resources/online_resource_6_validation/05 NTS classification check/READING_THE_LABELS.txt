HOW TO READ THE NTS CHECK

Y = the material examined supports that NTS area. NR = it was not reported in the material examined. U = the available information was insufficient to decide. NR does not claim that the full paper definitively contains no such skill; U is not silently changed to NR or exclusion.

Each of 44 records has nine judgements (396 in total). Only 38 are in the frozen 483-study corpus, giving 342 matched comparisons. Fifteen were unclear. Of the 327 determinate comparisons, 305 agreed and 22 differed. Both positive and negative agreements contribute to 93.3%; it is not 93.3% positive-skill detection accuracy.
