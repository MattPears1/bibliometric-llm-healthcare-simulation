ONLINE RESOURCE 4 - PHRASES AND NON-TECHNICAL SKILLS

The predefined phrase analysis uses 34 labels. Phrase_dictionary_and_counts.csv combines their exact variants with whole-study and title/abstract/keyword counts. Its two measures distinguish the main 483-study analysis from the 447-study retained-abstract sensitivity. Matching uses NFKC normalization, case-insensitive whole phrases and equivalent hyphens/spaces, without stemming or additional synonyms. The same phrase counts at most once per study; matches in several fields still count as one study. Absence of a match is not evidence of absence from an unexamined full text.

Study_phrase_indicators.csv has one row per study and one binary column per phrase: 1 means a retained title, abstract or source-keyword match; 0 means none. Phrase_cooccurrences.csv gives all 561 unordered pairs of the 34 phrases, including zero counts. It is calculated from these unchanged indicators. in_figure_3 identifies the 66 pairs among the 12 concepts displayed in the published figure; the dictionary marks those 12 concepts. cosine_strength is pair_count / sqrt(count_1 * count_2), blank if either frequency is zero. No centrality, community or causal interpretation is implied. This complete table replaces earlier conditional-network subsets, whose selected labels and thresholds differed from the final figure.

The separate fine-grained NTS thesaurus has 78 concept groups and 303 exact variants. It was applied to all 483 titles and 447 available abstracts, with Unicode normalization, lowercasing and standardized punctuation/hyphenation, binary whole counting, and no stemming or fuzzy matching. NTS_thesaurus_and_counts.csv combines the definitions and counts; NTS_study_assignments.csv lists the 617 observed study-concept matches. Join concept_id to the thesaurus and paper_id to Resource 5. NTS_cooccurrences.csv preserves the 171 upper-triangle entries, including 18 diagonal frequencies, for the 18 concepts in Figure 5. Off-diagonal values are same-study counts and cosine-normalised strengths.

These lexical detections are distinct from the nine substantively coded NTS domains: those require an educational objective, intervention component, assessment construct or outcome, not just a phrase. Domain totals are in Resource 3 and each study's coded domains in Resource 5. The locked structured-extraction codebook is included in Resource 5's data dictionary. Figures are supplied once in technical/figures, outside this ZIP.
