ONLINE RESOURCE 6 - PILOT AND LEAD-AUTHOR CHECKS

This resource contains five separate exercises, one CSV each. They are checks of studies and review decisions, not records about research participants. There was one human lead-author reviewer. Later decisions remain separate from the frozen 483-study analysis; no reclassification of the published dataset is implied.

1. Pilot: 100 AI-agent workflow-test records, sampled with seed 20260813-HV1. Initial decisions were 30 INCLUDE, 67 EXCLUDE and 3 UNCERTAIN. It tests workflow, not human-model agreement. The original H-prefixed Pilot template IDs are not the later human sample IDs: use paper_id for linking. The 23 overlapping human-sample records are identified in eligibility_sample1_overlap_id. The original protocol amendment and recovered-source hashes remain in the technical material.

2. Eligibility sample 1: the August 100-record stratified human check, with its original rationales merged into the decision/comparison file. Its initial comparison was with the then-current five-source pipeline, before Scopus integration: 85/100 matches (85.0%; exact 95% CI 76.47% to 91.35%). A planned second human check was not completed. Historical human_final_* fields are preserved as recorded; they are not the later September post-comparison answers.

3. Eligibility sample 2: the September 100-record check: 80 within the candidate frame and 20 outside it. Initial recorded agreement among firm within-frame answers was 65/77, with one blank and two uncertain answers. After separate clarification of the blank it was 65/78, still with two uncertain answers. After comparison/recheck it was 71/80, with nine differences. These are different stages, not interchangeable accuracy estimates. The 20 outside-frame records are not part of those denominators; V074 remained INCLUDE in the latest check. The five overlaps with Sample 1 are identified in the CSV; together the two human samples contain 195 unique identifiers, not 200 independent studies.

4. Disagreement recheck: 15 selected disagreements with the original and latest author decisions side by side. This is a selected subset, not a new random validation sample. Most final author rationales were not recorded; V040 has the explicit explanation that it was this manuscript's earlier preprint. Missing explanations are not replaced by model-written rationales.

5. NTS classification check: 44 studies with nine author-checked NTS labels and source/evidence notes. The 38 corpus studies also have frozen_* comparison columns (342 study-domain pairs). For the six other checked records, frozen_* cells are blank because there is no matched corpus comparison. Counts are 305 agreements, 22 differences and 15 unclear; 305/327 determinate comparisons agree (93.3%). Agreement includes non-reporting in both sources; it is not positive-skill detection accuracy. The 44 records were the union of frozen or later author inclusions within Sample 2, not an independent random sample.

REVIEW PROCESS AND FIELD DEFINITIONS
Sample 2 used seed 20260919 and five strata of 20. The lead author reports hiding AI columns for the initial answers; comparison/rechecking was later and was not blinded. Two supplementary GPT-6 Astra reviews at maximum reasoning were prepared before the human review was finished. They are separate from the original GPT-5.6 Sol production streams; supplementary_Astra_* rationales are model-authored.

Pilot P IDs, Sample 1 H IDs and Sample 2 V IDs identify records within exercises, not people. Use paper_id to join records; it is not necessarily a DOI. Bibliographic titles and URLs provide source context, not a new live availability check. sample_group, sample_component and stratum retain sampling groups; their results must not be pooled as population accuracy estimates.

INCLUDE and EXCLUDE are stage-specific eligibility decisions. UNCERTAIN is not automatic exclusion. BLANK_PENDING_CONFIRMATION is a missing initial answer. after_blank_clarification keeps subsequent confirmation separate. post_comparison/latest fields are later answers, not a second blinded pass. then_current_pipeline/historical_model fields refer to the original five-source comparison; frozen_production fields refer to the retained current comparison. production_A/B_initial_* are pre-resolution production answers. Primary codes are defined in Resource 2. Empty final rationale fields remain missing evidence.

For NTS, Y means supported in the examined material; NR means not reported there; U means insufficient information. NR does not prove absence from the full article. Unprefixed domain columns are the lead author's checks of AI-prepared evidence summaries/labels; frozen_* columns are production labels. For the 38 matched studies, U is UNCLEAR; otherwise equal labels AGREE and unequal labels DIFFER. Blank frozen_* cells for the other six records mean no matched corpus comparison, not NR. material_examined, source_locator and evidence_notes preserve source/version limitations and uncertainty. These were not independent blinded first-pass extractions. The original other-classifications sheet remains blank and is not presented as completed human validation.
