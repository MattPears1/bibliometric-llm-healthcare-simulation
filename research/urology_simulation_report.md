## 1. UROLOGY SIMULATION BOOT CAMPS

### 1.1 Major Boot Camps Worldwide

#### UK Urology Simulation Boot Camp (USBC)
- **Location:** Leeds, UK (annually since 2015)
- **Target:** Newly appointed UK urology registrars (ST3 level)
- **Status:** Since 2018, recommended to all new urology residents in the UK by the Joint Committee on Surgical Training (JCST)
- **Format:** Incorporates core technical and non-technical skills based on the JCST urological training curriculum
- **Key publication:** Ahmed K et al. "How to Implement a Simulation-Based Education Programme: Lessons from the UK Urology Simulation Boot Camp." *Indian Journal of Surgery* (2021). PMC8260348
- **Key publication:** Ahmed K et al. "The Success and Evolution of a Urological 'Boot Camp' for Newly Appointed UK Urology Registrars: Incorporating Simulation, Nontechnical Skills and Assessment." PMID: 31036524
- **Key publication:** "Urology simulation boot camp: A perspective from non-UK delegates." PMID: 32943271
- **Now in its 10th edition** (2025 listing on BAUS website)

#### AUA Fundamentals in Urology
- **Organizer:** American Urological Association
- **Format:** 1.5-day programme with simulations focused on clinical skills (priapism, catheters, bladder ultrasound, endoscopy) and interpersonal/communication skills
- **Assessment:** Pre-module quiz, post-module quiz, online learning modules, hands-on simulation sessions

#### AUA-Affiliated Medical Student Boot Camps
- **UPJ INSIGHT Advanced Urology Boot Camp:** Simulation-based curriculum to enhance 4th-year medical student procedural competency. Published: Acosta et al. (2023), PMID: 37103410
- **NY Section AUA EMPIRE Collaborative:** Hands-on urology intern boot camp focusing on robotics simulation, ultrasound skills, cystoscopy, suturing
- **Pilot Boot Camp:** Curriculum for common bedside urologic procedures (Foley catheter, cystoscopy, bladder irrigation). Published: PMID: 36002088

#### EAU / European School of Urology (ESU)
- **EUREP Hands-on Training (HOT) Courses:** Expert-led sessions using innovative simulators/models with 1:1:1 ratio (one station, one trainee, one tutor)
- **Robotic Training Curriculum:** 12-week programme including e-learning, 1 week structured simulation (VR, synthetic, animal, cadaveric platforms), and supervised modular training for robot-assisted radical prostatectomy (RARP). Published: *European Urology* (2014), PMID: 25454612
- **Endoscopic Stone Treatment (EST-s2/A) Curriculum:** 38-month development involving EAU Uro-technology and urolithiasis sections. Published: PMID: 34006138
- **ESU Urology Simulation Boot Camp:** Listed on the EAU/Uroweb training curriculum for first-year residents

#### BAUS-Specific Courses
- **Cadaveric Training Programme:** 3-day modular cadaveric operative urology training on fresh-frozen specimens, supervised by certified urological surgeons
- **Endourology Residential Operative Course:** Annual course for final-year trainees, limited to 4 delegates, one-on-one expert consultant supervision
- **Laparoscopic Training Course (with Dundee Institute):** Uses training animal tissue phantoms and VR simulation

### 1.2 Formats Used

| Format | Description | Examples |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Mannequin-based** | High-fidelity mannequins with task trainers | URO Mentor (VR + mannequin for ureteroscopy); SimMan integration for partial nephrectomy |
| **Standardized patients** | Trained actors for history-taking, communication, sensitive exams | Used for non-technical skills stations at USBC and AUA courses |
| **Virtual reality** | Computer-based environments for procedural training | Robotic surgery simulators, endoscopy simulators |
| **Cadaveric** | Fresh-frozen human specimens | BAUS 3-day modular course |
| **Low-fidelity bench models** | Cheap, accessible DIY models | Polyvinyl alcohol models, 3D-printed trainers |
| **Virtual/online** | Remote delivery breaking geographical barriers | Virtual boot camp reaching 16+ UK medical schools and 4 continents |

### 1.3 How Non-Technical Skills Are Currently Taught
- Full-immersion simulation in mock operating rooms
- Scenario-based sessions with trained actors/standardized patients
- The USBC specifically incorporates NTS stations alongside technical skills
- Formative feedback and debriefing sessions
- Case-based discussions and didactic teaching
- Communication skills stations (e.g., breaking bad news, consent)

### 1.4 Known Limitations of Current Boot Camps

**Cost:**
- UK USBC estimated at ~GBP 500 per participant
- 64% of US urology program directors agreed cost was a limiting factor for simulation curricula
- Training costs increasingly borne by trainees; study budgets often insufficient even for mandatory courses
- Advanced VR technology and patient-specific modelling further restrict accessibility

**Accessibility:**
- Only 53.3% of UK urology trainees have access to simulation facilities outside of course settings
- Geographic barriers for in-person attendance
- Virtual formats demonstrated as a solution (e.g., virtual boot camp with no financial cost to delegates)

**Implementation Challenges:**
- Complex implementation requiring considerable planning tailored to specific educational targets
- Need for constant technology updates
- Need for advanced planning and faculty willingness to participate
- Sustainability and reproducibility concerns

**Key review:** "Low-cost simulation models in Urology: a systematic review of the literature." PMC7587474 / PMID: 33133668

### 3.2 Role of Human Standardized Patients
- Patient actors represent real patients for practicing non-technical and cognitive skills
- Used for history-taking, physical examination, and communication scenarios
- Some standardized patients trained as models for genitourinary exams and ultrasounds
- In mannequin-based scenarios, human operators can speak through speakers/microphones to give the mannequin a "voice" and respond to trainee questions in real time

### 3.3 Replacing the Human with LLM + Text-to-Speech

**Technical Architecture (from published systems):**
The processing pipeline follows: Speech Recognition (STT) -> LLM Generation -> Text-to-Speech (TTS) + Animation

**Existing Prototypes and Systems:**
1. **GPT-3.5 + Mixed Reality Mannequin:** Applied to pulmonary training; overlays 3D avatar on physical mannequin integrating speech recognition, speech synthesis, and emotional expression
2. **CLiVR (Conversational Learning in VR):** Integrates LLMs, speech processing, and 3D avatars for realistic doctor-patient interactions. Published: arXiv:2510.19031
3. **Unity-based ICU Simulation:** 3D environment with 5 AI-powered avatars driven by LLM agents for adverse event investigation training. Published: arXiv:2508.04904
4. **EvoPatient Framework:** Multi-agent coevolution framework allowing LLMs to simulate standardized patients without human supervision. Published: arXiv:2412.11716
5. **AIPatient System:** User study with medical students showed high fidelity, usability, and educational value comparable to or better than human simulated patients for history taking

**Technical Feasibility Assessment:**
- TTS technology (e.g., ChatTTS, open-source models) now optimized for conversational tasks
- Speech-to-text transcription is mature and accurate
- LLM response generation is fast enough for near-real-time conversation
- Main challenges: latency management, emotional expression fidelity, integration with physical mannequin hardware, and ensuring clinical accuracy of responses
- **Key resource:** GitHub repository "Awesome-LLM-Patient-Simulators" - curated paper collection for LLM-based patient simulators

## 4. NON-TECHNICAL SKILLS IN UROLOGY

### 4.1 Communication Challenges Specific to Urology

**Sensitive Examinations:**
- Genital and rectal examinations require particular sensitivity and professionalism
- Patients frequently feel embarrassment or discomfort discussing urological conditions
- Providers must foster safe, non-judgmental environments for open communication

**Cancer Diagnosis Communication:**
- Prostate cancer, bladder cancer, kidney cancer diagnoses are common in urology
- Getting a cancer diagnosis becomes part of patients' identity
- AUA uses the **ARIA mnemonic**: Assess understanding/concerns, Reflect by listening with empathy, Inform by clarifying misconceptions
- **SPIKES six-step protocol** widely recommended for breaking bad news
- Key publication: Montgomery. "The practice of breaking bad news." *Trends in Urology & Men's Health* (2021). DOI: 10.1002/tre.785

**Treatment Decision Communication:**
- Complex shared decision-making for active surveillance vs. intervention
- Navigating concerns about sexual function, continence, fertility
- Pre-surgical counselling (e.g., vasectomy, TURP)

### 4.2 NTS Frameworks Applied to Urology

#### NoTSUS (Non-Technical Skills for Urological Surgeons)
- **THE urology-specific NTS framework**
- **Development:** International, longitudinal observational study using 3-round Delphi methodology
- **Categories:** Decision-making, leadership, teamwork, situational awareness
- **Validation:** 62 participants (43 trainees, 19 specialists); correlation with NoTSS scale: r = 0.93 and r = 0.88 for expert raters A and B
- **Published:** Ahmed K et al. "Non-technical skills for urological surgeons (NoTSUS): development and evaluation of curriculum and assessment scale." *World Journal of Urology* (2021). DOI: 10.1007/s00345-020-03406-6. PMID: 32809178. PMC8217036

#### Other Applicable Frameworks
- **NOTSS (Non-Technical Skills for Surgeons):** Generic surgical NTS framework applied to urology
- **NOTECHS:** Evaluates OR teams across 4 dimensions: (1) leadership/management, (2) teamwork/cooperation, (3) problem-solving/decision-making, (4) situation awareness
- **OTAS (Observational Teamwork Assessment for Surgery):** Refined and applied in urological surgery. PMID: 17487527

### 4.3 Key Reviews on NTS in Urology
- **"Communication, teamwork, and the role of non-technical skills in Urology: A review by the European School of Urology"** (2025) - *European Urology Education*. ScienceDirect. **Most recent comprehensive review.** Concludes there is still a lack of a validated, standardised NTS training programme directed at urologists applicable to all levels.
- **"Non-technical skills: a review of training and evaluation in urology"** - *World Journal of Urology* (2020). DOI: 10.1007/s00345-019-02920-6. PMC7303051. Highlights need for formal programme as mandatory part of training.
- **"Urology technical and non-technical skills development: the emerging role of simulation"** - PMID: 26695716

### 4.4 Teamwork and Leadership in Urological Surgery
- Crisis resource management (CRM) has established role in acute care; implementation in urology is important for intraoperative emergency situations
- Surgeon's leadership role critical in emergency situations with decision-making power and patient outcome responsibility
- Assessment of interprofessional teamwork in OR increasingly studied using observational methods

### 5.3 Expert Opinions on LLM-Mannequin Integration

**Current consensus from the literature:**
1. LLMs can replicate medical conditions and patient-doctor interactions with high fidelity and low cost
2. The multimodal pipeline (STT -> LLM -> TTS) is technically mature
3. User studies show LLM virtual patients are comparable to or better than human SPs for history taking
4. AI-driven simulations enable personalized feedback and adaptive learning environments
5. Automated debriefing systems can analyze performance and provide individualized insights

**Remaining Challenges:**
- Need for high-quality, specialty-specific training datasets
- Transition from simulated to real clinical environments
- Ethical implications: data privacy, algorithmic bias
- Ensuring clinical accuracy and avoiding hallucination in medical contexts
- Regulatory considerations for use in formal assessment
- Integration complexity with physical mannequin hardware
- Latency requirements for natural conversation flow

### 5.4 The Gap This Research Could Fill

Based on this landscape review, the following gap is clearly identifiable:

**No published study has specifically integrated an LLM with a physical urology mannequin to replace the human standardized patient for non-technical skills training in urology.**

The components exist separately:
- Validated urology mannequins (URO Mentor, task trainers)
- Validated NTS frameworks (NoTSUS)
- LLM virtual patient systems (various prototypes)
- TTS/STT technology (mature)
- Urology-specific boot camp curricula (USBC, AUA, EAU)
- LLM performance validated on urology knowledge (UroBot, ChatGPT on EBU exams)

But their integration for urology NTS training remains unpublished.
